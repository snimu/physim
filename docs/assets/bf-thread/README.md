# BF thread figures

Draft figures that follow BF through the write-up: the world, the apparatus, the
interventions, and what the evaluation measures. Generated by
`scripts/render_bf_thread.py` from native simulations of the recorded BF
preparation; no model inference. `provenance.json` records inputs, runs, checks,
display scales and video encodings.

```sh
PYTHONPATH=environments/physim:packages/blobkit python scripts/render_bf_thread.py
```

Runs are cached under `outputs/bf-thread-20261002` (about 40 MB each). `--only`
renders a subset; `--layout desktop|mobile|both` selects variants. Static figures
take about two minutes including the runs; each video takes several minutes.

## One way of showing the world

- One panel per field, always in genome order: u₀, x₀, x₁, x₂. Desktop figures put
  the four panels in a row; `-mobile` variants reflow the same panels into a 2 × 2
  grid at the same panel size.
- Each field keeps one identity hue and one fixed color scale in every figure.
  White is the resting value; darker means larger.
  - u₀ excitation, blue, −0.7 to 1.1
  - x₀ slow inhibitor, aqua, 0 to 1.05
  - x₁ fast inhibitor, violet, 0 to 0.55
  - x₂ trail, orange, 0 to 0.25 on an asinh scale (linear below about 0.004),
    so natural trails near 0.02 and source pulses near 0.25 are both readable.
  The four hues are validated categorical slots: all pairs stay distinguishable
  under protanopia and deuteranopia (ΔE ≥ 9.2) and for normal vision (ΔE ≥ 16.3).
- Apparatus glyphs are identical on every panel because every sensor reads every
  field: device 0 sensors are circles, device 1 sensors are squares, the source is
  a plus. While a pulse runs, a dashed circle of radius σ = 2 marks its launch
  position on the field it injects into, and the plus takes that field's hue.
  After the pulse ends, a faint dotted circle marks where it ran.
- Two views: the world view (56 × 56 units, 6 units above and 4 to the right of the
  devices) and the lab view (32 × 32 units centered on the devices). The world
  figure marks the lab view with a dashed square.
- Experiment figures draw the device the experiment uses (device 0).

## Checks before drawing

- The genome and preparation are registry artifacts verified by SHA-256. The
  preparation's field hash matches the published BF bundle manifest, and its u₀
  values match the crop in `docs_source/data/world-visuals.npz` exactly.
- The apparatus is rebuilt with the BF rule in `generators/physim/eval_preparation.py`.
- Device-0 u₀ readings for the coupled and feedback-removed sham and trail-pulse
  runs equal `docs_source/data/bf-evaluation.npz` bit for bit at all eight times.
- Readings computed from the stored crops equal the oracle's own device-0 queries.
- With the x₂ → u₀ term removed, u₀, x₀ and x₁ stay bitwise identical with and
  without the pulse. With it, u₀ at device 0's sensors differs by 0.754 RMS at
  t = 50 for this realization (recorded mean over three: 0.755).
- The 300-unit continuation uses the construction and seed of
  `scripts/render_world_movies.py`. A one-time comparison outside the script:
  re-rendered in the old style, its t = 250 frame matches the published film within
  encoding error (mean absolute pixel difference 1.7 of 255).

The figures have white backgrounds and are designed for the full 820-pixel column.
One CSS rule keeps video letterboxing white:
`.bf-figure video{background:#fff}`.
