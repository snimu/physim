A group selects readings to score jointly, such as one field at several sensors and times. Every BF experiment is scored on the same four groups, listed below. For a fixed experiment and group, divide forecast readings fik and observed readings ojk by predeclared positive scales sk in their native units:

xik=fiksk , yjk=ojksk

The scales sk set the relative weight of the readings. A smaller scale makes the same absolute prediction error count more. Scales can account for expected differences in field magnitudes, or emphasize a field whose response an experiment is designed to detect. They are chosen before evaluating predictors and remain fixed for that experiment. In BF, an error of 0.05 in the trail field x₂ counts as much as an error of 1 in u₀, matching the trail’s much smaller values.

For these dimensionless vectors with d coordinates, define the root mean square distance:

D(a,b)= 1d∑k=1d(ak−bk)2

With M predicted samples and N independent simulated realizations, the energy score for group g in experiment c is:

Ecg= 1MN ∑i=1M ∑j=1N D(xi,yj)− 12M2 ∑i=1M ∑ℓ=1M D(xi,xℓ)

Average equally over the Gc groups in each experiment, then over the C experiments. For BF, Gc = 4 for every experiment and C = 15:

Sc=1Gc ∑g=1GcEcg , S=1C∑c=1CSc

BF uses M = 64 and N = 2. Apply the reward mapping once, to the mean energy S:

RK(S)= max(0,min(−log10S,K))K

At S = 0, define RK = 1. A missing predictor or a failed prediction contract receives zero reward.
